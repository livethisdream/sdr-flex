How many surfaces?

The layout study got chrome down to about 90 px and then stopped paying. What is left to win is not height — it is the number of separate things on screen and the number of different menus behind them. This one is clickable, because a popunder and a hover are behaviors and a screenshot cannot say whether they feel worse than what they replace.

Try: click a crumb for the map · hover one for its values · i pins that card open · drag a box on the spectrum for operations · click the spectrum without dragging for view options · right-click for the node's own menu · space pauses.

What it costs and what it buys

Struck-through rows are surfaces that stop existing. Nothing here is free: the two rows that disappear were also the inventory of what you had built, and the strip was the only place the auto/manual edge was visible without asking.

What it breaks

Three of these are real costs against rules already written down, not hypotheticals. Two have answers in the prototype; one does not yet.

Law 12 — “what you need mid-gesture stays visible. You cannot hover while dragging.” Hover-summoned values vanish the moment a drag starts, which is exactly when you are watching them. answered: i pins the card and it survives the drag
