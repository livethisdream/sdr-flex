A threshold is a line on the waveform

The last unbuilt piece. Everything else in the strip found a home — the tuner's numbers to a summoned card, the view's choices to the menu, typed fields and a plugin's knobs to the card as well. What was left was the twenty numeric node parameters and the claim that they belong on the object, which had been described four times and never built. That claim was the only reason the bottom bar was still alive.

This runs the real slicer. web/src/dsp.js has no imports, so the page imports otsuThreshold, estimateSymbolPeriod and pwmSlice from it directly. Drag the line and the bits below are the bits the app would actually produce — including the ones it would get wrong.

Try: drag the dashed line up and down · drag the handle at the foot to set the symbol period · hover either for the evidence behind its auto value · ⟲ auto on the tag puts both back.

The evidence ADR-0017 wants a tooltip on the cell a histogram beside the line, on hover
A symbol period a number in µs a spacing, which walks off the pulse edges when wrong

The census's hard case has an answer, and it is a rule rather than a workaround: the parameter goes on the plot of its input, never its output. That is why Bits, Bytes and Events having nothing to draw on turns out not to matter — a slicer's threshold was never going to live over its own bytes. It belongs over the envelope being sliced, one pane up, which is drawn already.

What it does not settle. This is one parameter of one node, and the easiest one: a scalar with a natural axis to sit on. A de-emphasis time constant, a BFO offset, a volume — those are numeric too and have no such axis, and nothing here shows where they go. The honest scope is that threshold and symbol period work on the object, which is two of twenty. The rest is still a claim.
